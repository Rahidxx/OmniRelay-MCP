# OmniRelay MCP – Use Cases

## 1. Smart Bill and Split Autopilot

Goal: Simplify bill management and expense splitting.

Proposed workflow:
1. Detect a bill from an email.
2. Extract relevant information.
3. Calculate individual shares.
4. Request user approval.
5. Prepare expense records and reminders.

## 2. Appointment and Commute Sync

Goal: Connect appointment information with calendar and commute planning.

Proposed workflow:
1. Identify appointment details from a message.
2. Extract date, time and location.
3. Prepare a calendar event.
4. Retrieve route information.
5. Ask for approval before taking actions.

## 3. Document and Deadline Concierge

Goal: Make document management and deadline tracking easier.

Proposed workflow:
1. Identify important documents.
2. Extract dates and relevant information.
3. Prepare tasks and reminders.
4. Draft follow-up emails.
5. Let the user review before execution.

## Key Benefits
- Reduces repetitive manual work.
- Connects workflows across applications.
- Keeps users in control.
- Uses structured and permission-scoped actions.
